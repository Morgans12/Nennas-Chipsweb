FROM FARM TO BAG

How We Make the
Perfect Crunch

It's not just a potato chip; it's a small-batch masterpiece. Follow the journey of our chips from the rolling hills to your favorite snack bowl.

1

Farm Fresh Potatoes

We partner with family farms that prioritize soil health. Every potato is hand-selected for its high starch content—the secret to that legendary Chip's crunch. No GMOs, just pure, earthy goodness.

eco Locally Sourced psychology Heirloom Varieties
2

The Perfect Slice

Thin enough to be crisp, thick enough to hold our bold seasonings. Our proprietary "V-Cut" precision slicers ensure every chip has maximum surface area for flavor adhesion and structural integrity.

"Precision isn't just a metric; it's the difference between a snack and an experience."

3

Small-Batch Kettle Cooking

We don't do mass production. Each batch is fried in small kettles at fluctuating temperatures. This irregular cooking method is what gives our chips their unique, non-uniform shapes and that signature hard-bite crunch.

  • check_circle High-Oleic Sunflower Oil for a clean finish
  • check_circle Temperature-controlled for zero burn
4

Flavor Explosion

This is where the magic happens. While the chips are still warm, we tumble them in our bespoke spice blends. From Smoky BBQ to Zesty Lime, every grain of seasoning is designed to wake up your taste buds.
