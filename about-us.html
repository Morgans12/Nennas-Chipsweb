It All Started With a Single Bite.

Way before the big bags and the fancy flavors, there was just a tiny kitchen in Brooklyn, a massive bag of potatoes, and a quest for the ultimate crunch.

HAND
CRAFTED

Make Every Moment Crunchy.

Our founder, "Chip" (yes, that's his real nickname), believed that snacks should be more than just filler—they should be an event. He spent six months perfecting the "Double-Fry" technique in his mother's kitchen, testing over 40 varieties of sea salt.

Today, we've moved to a bigger space, but the spirit remains the same. We don't just sell chips; we sell that satisfying, earth-shaking, conversation-stopping crunch that makes you forget everything else.
