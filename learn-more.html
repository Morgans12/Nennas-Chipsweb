The Dietary Lowdown

check_circle Are they Gluten-Free?

Absolutely. Our facility is certified 100% gluten-free. We never use wheat, barley, or rye in any of our seasonings.

check_circle Non-GMO Project Verified?

Yes. We strictly source non-GMO potatoes and oils. We believe in keeping it real and natural.

check_circle Are they Vegan?

The majority of our flavors are 100% plant-based. Check the individual flavor pages for specifics, but even our 'Cheese' uses nutritional yeast!

check_circle Zero Artificial Preservatives?

You bet. We use nitrogen-flushed bags and natural Vitamin E (Tocopherols) to keep things fresh the right way.
